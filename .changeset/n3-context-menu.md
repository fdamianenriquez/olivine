Add ContextMenu: wraps an area and opens a Dropdown-style menu (actions, checkable items, radio groups, submenus, headers, dividers) on right-click, touch long-press, Shift+F10 or the ContextMenu key. The menu sits on the pointer, or hangs from the focused element when opened by keyboard. The browser's menu stays in text fields, with Shift+right-click, and when disabled or empty. New locale entry `contextMenu`.
