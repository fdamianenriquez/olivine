Typography v2: titles in Big Shoulders Display, interface and reading text in Familjen Grotesk, code and data in Xanh Mono, kanji in Yuji Syuku (opt-in `olivine/fonts-jp.css`); amounts stay in Sprat Condensed. Labels, buttons, badges, tabs and meta lines are sentence case with no letter-spacing (nothing is uppercased outside stamps), table amount columns use the figure face at 16px, glyphs use the label face, and the active index of Nav and Accordion is ink. New type styles `label-s` and `figure-xs`; the `--font-serif` family is replaced by `--font-heading` and `--font-label`. Redaction, Ronzino, Martian Mono and Zen Old Mincho are no longer shipped.
