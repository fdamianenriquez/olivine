`useIndicator` gains `axis: "block"`: a stroke that travels along a vertical list with two edges timed apart (`.ol-indicator--block`, `--ol-indicator-size`, `--ol-indicator-color`). The default inline axis is unchanged. The `.ol-anchor` and `.ol-has-bottomnav` utilities and the disclosure fold (`.ol-fold`, used by Sidebar and TreeView) come with the navigation batch. ButtonGroup and SplitButton now overlap their borders with logical margins, so right-to-left bars keep one shared hairline.
