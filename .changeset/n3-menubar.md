Add Menubar: the horizontal application bar (File, Edit, View, Help). Each entry opens a Dropdown-style menu (actions, checkable items, radio groups, one submenu level) or runs an action. One tab stop, arrow-key movement between entries and open menus, hover and tap switching, controlled or uncontrolled open entry, and menus that hang from the bar and switch instantly. New locale entry `menubar`.
