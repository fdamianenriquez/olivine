Add BottomNav: a fixed bottom bar of 3 to 5 destinations for compact screens, with the same item shape as NavigationRail. It adds an opt-in hide on scroll, an opt-in `scrollHint` (a small ↓ tab while more content is below), a `contained` mode and the `--ol-dock` property that lifts a Fab above it. New locale entry `bottomNavLabel`. The Fab now rides above a BottomNav.
