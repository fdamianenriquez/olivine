Add TableOfContents: an "On this page" index of a long document, with nested links, a scroll-spy and a 3px olivo stroke that travels along a hairline rail. Also adds the `useHeadings` hook, which builds the list from the headings of a rendered page. `aria-current="location"` marks the current entry. New locale entry `tocLabel`. The block-axis `useIndicator` and the `.ol-anchor` utility come with it.
