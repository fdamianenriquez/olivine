Tabs: new `activation` (`automatic` | `manual`), `orientation` (`horizontal` | `vertical`) and `keepMounted` (default `true`) props; scroll buttons when the tabs overflow; the selected tab scrolls into view; the olive edge slides between tabs on the motion tokens; a disabled or out-of-range value falls back to the first enabled tab; only the visible panel is a tab stop, and only when it has nothing focusable.
