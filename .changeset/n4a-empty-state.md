Add EmptyState: the message that replaces a region with nothing to show, with a title, an optional description, a decorative mark and at most one action. Three variants carry default copy from the locale, and an opt-in live region announces it. New locale keys `emptyNoDataTitle`, `emptyNoResultsTitle`, `emptyNoResultsText`, `emptyErrorTitle` and `emptyErrorText`.
