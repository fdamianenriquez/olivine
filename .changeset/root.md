Add the `Root` component and the locale system: `Root` sets the Olivine ground, an optional `theme` (`washi`, `sumi`, or `auto` to follow the system) and the `locale` (`en`, `es`, `ja` or a full dictionary) with `labels` overrides. `locales`, `makeLabels`, `useLabels` and the `OlivineLabels` type are exported.
